Movie 1 : Avatar

"Avatar" refers to both a Hindu concept of divine incarnation and a major James Cameron sci-fi franchise, most famously the 2009 film about blue-skinned Na'vi on the moon Pandora, where humans use genetically engineered Na'vi bodies (Avatars) to interact with them, exploring themes of colonialism, environmentalism, and indigenous culture. The term literally means "descent" in Sanskrit, signifying a deity taking earthly form, tying into the franchise's depiction of humans descending to Pandora.

Movie 2 : Titanic

The RMS Titanic was a British luxury liner that famously sank on its maiden voyage in April 1912 after hitting an iceberg, resulting in the deaths of over 1,500 people and leading to major maritime safety reforms Known as the largest and most luxurious ship of its time, it was considered "unsinkable" but struck an iceberg on April 14, 1912, breaking apart and sinking in the early hours of April 15, 1912. The disaster sparked investigations,changed safety regulations, and cemented the ship's place in popular culture.

Movie 3 : Avengers

The Avengers are Marvel's premier superhero team ,formed by S.H.I.E.L.D. to counter threats too big for single heroes, notably Loki's Chitauri invasion, uniting Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow, and Hawkeye initially. Their history involves growth, internal conflicts (like the Sokovia Accords splitting them), major battles against villains like Ultron and Thanos,and evolving rosters, becoming "Earth's Mightiest Heroes" defending against global and cosmic dangers,both in comics (Earth-616) and the popular Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU).
